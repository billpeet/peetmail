# Signatures

Each account can have its own signature. PeetMail adds it under new messages, and under replies and forwards too if you choose.

## Setting one up

1. Open **Settings** (**Ctrl+,**) and choose **Mail**.
2. Under **Signatures**, type your signature for each account. **Ctrl+B**, **Ctrl+I** and **Ctrl+U** format text, and **Ctrl+K** adds a link.
3. Tick **Add to replies and forwards too** if you want it there as well.
4. Click **Save signatures**.

Pictures and the colours or fonts of a signature pasted from Outlook aren't kept yet.

## In the composer

The signature shows under what you type. It goes above the quoted message when you reply. You can change it for one message by clicking into it and typing; that doesn't change the saved one.

**Ctrl+Shift+S** takes it off the message you're writing, or puts it back. If it isn't there (on a reply, say), the **Signature** button next to **Send later** adds it.

When you switch the **From** account on a new message, the signature switches to that account's, unless you've taken it off.

## When your organisation adds signatures (CodeTwo, Exclaimer)

Some organisations add everyone's signature on their mail server, with a service like CodeTwo or Exclaimer. That works for mail you send from PeetMail too, because it goes out through your organisation's server like mail from Outlook does.

For that account, tick **My organisation adds my signature**. PeetMail then adds none, so the people you write to don't get two. The composer says so under your message.

You won't see that signature while you write, and it may not appear in your Sent folder: it's added after the message leaves PeetMail. Your organisation's settings decide where it goes in a reply, usually under your reply and above the quoted message.
